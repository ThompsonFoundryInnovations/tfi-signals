How stories are chosen

  • Up to five at a time. We would rather show a few that matter than many that do not.
  • Current and relevant. Technology, online visibility, funding, business operations and regulatory developments that affect independent businesses.
  • Sourced. Every story links to the original source, and we show the date we last verified it.
  • Plain. Each has a short summary, why it matters to an owner, and one practical next step.
