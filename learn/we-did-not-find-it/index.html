What "we did not find it" means

You will see this wording throughout a Signals report. It is a plain statement about what we saw.

Last updated: October 10, 2026 Source: how TFI Signals works today About a 4 minute read

Signals is still being designed and built. This guide describes the report as it works today. Some things will change and new features are being added soon, and this guide will be updated to match.

What it means

We looked and did not find it

Every report says it plainly: "Treat 'not found' as 'we did not find it,' not as proof that it does not exist." It is a statement about what one check saw, not a warning and not a verdict.

Where you will see it

In the summary

For example: "No Google Maps listing was found for this business by this check." or "No phone number was found by these checks."

In the count at the bottom

The report counts public checks that "found nothing." Those checks ran and did not find anything.

Why a listing might not show up

There is no listing

The business may never have been added to that source.

It exists but is not visible to public search

Some listings can only be seen after signing in, or are hidden from search tools.

The source cannot be read directly

Pages such as Facebook and LinkedIn cannot be read directly. A profile that is not shown may simply be one we could not see.

The name or address is written differently

A listing under an older name, an abbreviation or a different address may not look like a match.

"Found nothing" is different from "could not be checked"

Found nothing

The check ran, looked, and did not find it.

Could not be checked

We could not get an answer at all, so we say nothing about whether it exists.

What you can do

  1. Search for yourself. Open the source and search your business name and city. If you find a listing, check that the details are right.
  2. Decide if it matters to you. Whether to be listed somewhere is your decision. The main sources are the ones most customers use.
  3. If you want a listing, use the guides for claiming and adding one, linked below.
  4. If something we reported is wrong, tell us. The report has a feedback link at the bottom, and corrections help us understand where sources are out of date.

Want to see one for your business? Type in a business name and city. A check usually takes one to three minutes.
